FLIP IT! for Android (package com.flipit.puzzle)

Effective 16 November 2026

What this game is

FLIP IT! is a free puzzle you can play without an account. Galasoft M.G. does not run a game server and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal details.

Information stored on your phone

The game saves your progress only on the device. That save includes:

  • the highest level you have unlocked
  • stars and fewest moves for completed levels
  • daily challenge results
  • your settings for sound, music, vibration, and theme
  • an unfinished level, so you can continue it
  • whether you have already seen the tutorial

The game does not upload this save. Reset progress in Settings deletes the saved levels and results. Uninstalling the app deletes the save as well. Settings and the fact that you saw the tutorial stay on the device if you reset progress from inside the game.

Ads

The game uses the internet to show ads from Google AdMob. You may see a banner, a full-screen ad after every fifth regular level, and an optional rewarded ad when you ask for extra moves or extra hints. Closing a rewarded ad early does not grant the reward.

Where Google requires it, a consent form appears before ads start. You can use that form to choose how Google uses data for ads. Google may process the advertising ID and other device data under its own rules. Those requests go to Google, not to a Galasoft M.G. server.

Google’s policies are at policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

What the game does not access

The game plays sound effects and music on the device, and it can vibrate while you play if vibration is turned on. It does not use the microphone, camera, location, or contacts, and it does not read your photos or files.

Children

FLIP IT! is not directed at children under 13. The game does not ask for a name, email address, or other personal details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
